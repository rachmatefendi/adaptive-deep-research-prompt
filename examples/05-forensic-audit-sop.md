# Use case 05 — Forensic audit and structured-financing SOP research

**Purpose:** Inform operational control design from public guidance and any supplied internal records.

## Ready-to-paste research brief

Use with [`PROMPT.md`](../PROMPT.md).

```text
RESEARCH TOPIC:
Forensic verification and risk-monitoring procedures for
structured financing backed by contracts, purchase orders,
and trade finance documents in Indonesia.

Research Objective:
Research appropriate verification procedures, fraud-risk
indicators, source authenticity checks, exception handling,
and governance controls suitable for a draft internal SOP.

Expected Outcome: FORENSIC
Target Audience: AUDITORS
Research Depth: COMPREHENSIVE
Geographic Scope / Jurisdiction: INDONESIA
Research Time Horizon: CURRENT
Output Language: BAHASA INDONESIA
Writing Style: FORMAL
Evidence Standard: HIGH-ASSURANCE RESEARCH
Governance Profile: MATERIAL_DECISION_RESEARCH
Deliverable Format: SOP

Additional Requirements:
Cover documentary provenance, obligor/end-user confirmations,
transaction trails, separation of duties, risk-based sample
selection, escalation, audit evidence, and residual risk.
Distinguish recommended controls from legal requirements.
Do not claim to have authenticated a transaction or detected
fraud without actual forensic evidence. Define the preconditions
for applying each step and the unresolved evidence gaps.
```

## Expected output characteristics

An evidence-backed SOP proposal with RACI candidates, controls, exception paths, verification checkpoints, and a clear distinction between researched methods and approved company policy.

**Critical watch-out:** A generated SOP is a draft recommendation, not an adopted mandatory control standard or completed audit.
