# Use case 08 — Comparative public-policy research

**Purpose:** Compare policy designs without importing another country's law or outcomes into Indonesia without adjustment.

## Ready-to-paste research brief

Use with [`PROMPT.md`](../PROMPT.md).

```text
RESEARCH TOPIC:
Comparative public-policy approaches to protecting small
businesses from digital payment fraud.

Research Objective:
Compare relevant regulatory and operational interventions,
identify their evidence of effectiveness, distributional impacts,
and implementation trade-offs for an Indonesian policy audience.

Expected Outcome: POLICY
Target Audience: REGULATORS
Research Depth: COMPREHENSIVE
Geographic Scope / Jurisdiction: INDONESIA plus selected comparative jurisdictions
Research Time Horizon: CURRENT and evidence-backed historical evaluations
Output Language: BAHASA INDONESIA
Writing Style: FORMAL
Evidence Standard: REGULATORY
Governance Profile: STANDARD_RESEARCH
Deliverable Format: WHITE PAPER

Additional Requirements:
Use original policy documents and independent evaluations
where available. Distinguish measured outcomes from policy
announcements. Assess institutional capacity, incentives,
operational cost, privacy implications, and unintended effects.
Do not assume foreign interventions transfer directly to
Indonesia. Identify what local validation would be required.
```

## Expected output characteristics

Policy objective, comparable intervention designs, relevant outcome evidence, uncertainty, local applicability, and staged recommendations without claiming authority to promulgate policy.

**Critical watch-out:** Research for a regulator is not equivalent to a regulator-endorsed position, enacted rule, or official interpretation.
