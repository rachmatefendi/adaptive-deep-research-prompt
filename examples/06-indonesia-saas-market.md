# Use case 06 — Indonesia SaaS market and go-to-market research

**Purpose:** Research a commercial hypothesis, rather than produce an invented TAM or executive-ready optimism.

## Ready-to-paste research brief

Use with [`PROMPT.md`](../PROMPT.md).

```text
RESEARCH TOPIC:
Commercial feasibility of conversational commerce automation
for small and mid-sized merchants in Indonesia.

Research Objective:
Assess identifiable demand drivers, customer workflows,
competition, credible addressable segments, pricing evidence,
distribution constraints, support economics, and defensibility.

Expected Outcome: BUSINESS
Target Audience: EXECUTIVES
Research Depth: COMPREHENSIVE
Geographic Scope / Jurisdiction: INDONESIA
Research Time Horizon: CURRENT
Output Language: BAHASA INDONESIA
Writing Style: EXECUTIVE
Evidence Standard: STANDARD
Governance Profile: MATERIAL_DECISION_RESEARCH
Deliverable Format: RESEARCH REPORT

Additional Requirements:
Separate observed market figures, transparent estimates, and
assumptions. Do not use generic global market multiples as an
Indonesian TAM. Examine onboarding, payment integration,
retention and support constraints. Include counter-hypotheses,
conservative unit-economics scenarios, and validation experiments.
```

## Expected output characteristics

A segmented market view, explicit data limitations, defensible economics framework, operational constraints, competitor differentiation, and stage-gated validation proposals.

**Critical watch-out:** Market size and churn/retention assumptions need defensible local evidence or must remain labeled scenarios.
