# Use case 01 — Academic literature review

**Purpose:** Illustrate evidence-driven academic synthesis without falsely claiming a systematic review.

## Ready-to-paste research brief

Use this request together with the full [`PROMPT.md`](../PROMPT.md).

```text
RESEARCH TOPIC:
Evidence on the effectiveness and limitations of generative AI
assistants for formative feedback in higher education.

Research Objective:
Identify what credible empirical studies support, which results
remain disputed, which study designs dominate, and which research
gaps materially affect adoption decisions.

Expected Outcome: ACADEMIC
Target Audience: ACADEMICS
Research Depth: COMPREHENSIVE
Geographic Scope / Jurisdiction: GLOBAL
Research Time Horizon: CURRENT, with a clear search as-of date
Output Language: ENGLISH
Writing Style: ACADEMIC
Evidence Standard: ACADEMIC
Governance Profile: STANDARD_RESEARCH
Deliverable Format: JOURNAL-STYLE PAPER

Additional Requirements:
Distinguish systematic reviews, randomized studies, observational
studies, and practitioner reports. Do not claim to perform a formal
systematic review without documenting a reproducible search and
selection process. Compare contradictory results and disclose
inaccessible full texts. Include methodology and research gaps.
```

## Expected output characteristics

A question-led literature synthesis, transparent search scope, study-design differentiation, contradictory findings, source register, and calibrated limitations.

**Critical watch-out:** An abstract is only partially accessed; it cannot justify verified claims about methodological details available exclusively in the full text. This is a prompt example, not an executed academic review.
