# Use case 03 — Structured financing investment assessment

**Purpose:** Research evidence and downside factors relevant to an investment/credit committee; not an authorization.

## Ready-to-paste research brief

Use with [`PROMPT.md`](../PROMPT.md).

```text
RESEARCH TOPIC:
Bankability, return drivers, and downside risk in Indonesian
project financing supported by long-term concession agreements.

Research Objective:
Develop an evidence-driven decision-support research report
comparing material project, cash-flow, contractual, counterparty,
security, and recovery risks.

Expected Outcome: INVESTMENT
Target Audience: INVESTORS
Research Depth: EXHAUSTIVE
Geographic Scope / Jurisdiction: INDONESIA
Research Time Horizon: CURRENT and relevant historical cases
Output Language: BAHASA INDONESIA
Writing Style: EXECUTIVE
Evidence Standard: HIGH-ASSURANCE RESEARCH
Governance Profile: MATERIAL_DECISION_RESEARCH
Deliverable Format: DECISION MEMO

Additional Requirements:
Assess debt service and cash flow logic, base/downside/severe
scenarios when data permits, obligor and offtaker dependencies,
contract enforceability considerations, completion risk,
and collateral/recovery limitations. State all assumptions.
Avoid invented market yields, discount rates, recovery rates,
credit probabilities, and regulatory requirements. Explicitly
separate investment analysis from committee approval.
```

## Expected output characteristics

Decision question, source-based market and legal observations, cash-flow sensitivities where supportable, downside pathways, exposure, open due-diligence questions, and nonbinding recommendations.

**Critical watch-out:** Without an actual term sheet or financial model, the output is thematic diligence guidance rather than a transaction-specific valuation or credit approval.
