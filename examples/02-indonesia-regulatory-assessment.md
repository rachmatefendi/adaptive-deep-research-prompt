# Use case 02 — Indonesian regulatory assessment

**Purpose:** Assess changing legal/regulatory conditions without treating proposals or journalism as binding law.

## Ready-to-paste research brief

Use with [`PROMPT.md`](../PROMPT.md).

```text
RESEARCH TOPIC:
Regulatory considerations for AI-enabled financial information
services offered to businesses in Indonesia.

Research Objective:
Map the potentially relevant Indonesian legal and regulatory
frameworks, identify role-dependent compliance implications,
and distinguish mandatory duties from interpretive questions.

Research Context:
A proposed product may process business financial data and
provide AI-generated analytics. It is not yet a licensed or
operational financial institution, and the exact service boundaries
must be assessed rather than assumed.

Expected Outcome: REGULATORY
Target Audience: EXECUTIVES
Research Depth: COMPREHENSIVE
Geographic Scope / Jurisdiction: INDONESIA
Research Time Horizon: CURRENT
Output Language: BAHASA INDONESIA
Writing Style: FORMAL
Evidence Standard: REGULATORY
Governance Profile: MATERIAL_DECISION_RESEARCH
Deliverable Format: RESEARCH REPORT

Excluded Scope:
Do not issue a binding legal opinion, conclude license eligibility
without sufficient facts, or claim regulatory approval.

Additional Requirements:
Verify official effective regulations, amendments, effective dates,
and regulator jurisdiction using current primary sources.
Separate written law, interpretation, regulator guidance, practice,
possible enforcement exposure, and evidence gaps.
```

## Expected output characteristics

A scoped regulatory map, source-linked effective status, application conditions, ambiguity register, and matters requiring counsel or regulator confirmation.

**Critical watch-out:** Do not infer specific law numbers or applicability if original texts were not accessed. This example contains no legal conclusions.
